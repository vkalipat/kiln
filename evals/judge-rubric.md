# Calibration labeller rubric

Choose the best and worst ideas in each four-item group by value alone, using the supplied shape-specific Value block. Best and worst must be different items; the two middle items remain unlabelled.

Treat each rendered dossier as the complete evidence base. Compare the importance and reach of the problem, the consequence if the stated claim or mechanism works, and the specificity of the benefit to the named audience or user. Do not reward prose polish, length, confidence, position, implementation ease, or novelty by itself. When evidence is incomplete, judge the value of what is actually stated rather than inventing missing support.
